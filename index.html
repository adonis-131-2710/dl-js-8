The Story of a Seagull and the Cat Who Taught Her to Fly - Luis Sepúlveda

“There’s a school of herring near the harbor!” the scout gull announced, and the flock of birds from the Red Sand Lighthouse responded with joyful, long cries.

They were flying over the Elber River, where the waters flow into the North Sea. From the air, they saw ships following one another like a pod of patient and disciplined whales waiting their turn to rush out into the open sea. Once at sea, the ships would determine their course and then spread out to every port on the planet.
